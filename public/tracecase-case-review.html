TraceCase: two fictional case reviews

AI-assisted project walkthrough. This explains the supplied synthetic scenarios; it is not a candidate-completed assessment, professional investigation or regulatory filing.

C001: unverified collections

Trigger and context: A salary-only customer profile declares monthly income of INR 30,000. The review period contains INR 1,200,000 in non-salary credits from ten payers. Closely following debits produce a temporal ratio of 95%.

Evidence assessment: The customer says the funds are collections for friends. That explanation is unverified. The relationships between the customer, payers and recipients and the economic purpose remain undocumented.

Reasonable next step: Request corroborating records explaining the parties, purpose and onward transfers. Escalate unresolved concerns internally under the applicable employer procedure. Immediate internal escalation may also be reasonable if the rationale is documented. The figures alone do not prove money laundering.

C002: property sale and mortgage redemption

Trigger and context: Non-routine property sale receipts total INR 1,200,000. An onward mortgage redemption totals INR 1,140,000. The temporal ratio is again 95%; the credits are 30 times the declared monthly income.

Evidence assessment: The scenario supplies fictional verified sale and mortgage records. Check that the buyers, beneficiary, dates and amounts match the transaction records. The verification assertions exist only within the exercise.

Reasonable next step: Closure of this particular training alert can be justified if the matching records explain the activity and no material contradiction remains. Document residual limitations. This does not permanently clear the customer relationship.

What changes the conclusion?

The two cases have similar transaction metrics. The quality and relevance of corroborating evidence differ. A high income multiple or temporal ratio starts questions; it does not determine guilt. The ratio does not trace fungible funds. Neither case includes live sanctions screening, registry verification or real customer data.
